Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How do I request a resume review?

Click the resume review button, complete the Google Form, and upload your CV.

Which resume formats can I submit?

Please follow the file format and size restrictions shown in the Google Form upload field.

Do I need a Google account to upload my CV?

Google Forms file uploads generally require you to sign in to a Google account. This is a Google Forms requirement.

Will my resume be publicly visible?

Your uploaded resume is submitted privately through Google Forms. CV Mentor should keep the response spreadsheet and uploaded files private.

Are other CV Mentor services available?

We're currently focusing on resume reviews. Additional career services may be introduced in the future.
